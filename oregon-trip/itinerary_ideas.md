# Itinerary Ideas & Swaps — Dog-Aware (2 small leashed dogs)

Research compiled June 2026 for the July 22–26, 2026 trip. **Re-confirm the ⚠️ items the week before you go** — coast conditions and permits change.

---

## 🐾 Dog ground rules for this coast (the good news)
- **Cannon Beach city beach, Seaside, Rockaway, Manzanita:** dogs allowed **OFF-LEASH under voice control** (or leashed). This is the headline perk — your dogs can actually run on the main Cannon Beach sand.
- **Haystack Rock Marine Garden:** dogs **must be leashed** right around the rock (protects tide pools + nesting birds). Off-leash is fine elsewhere on the beach.
- **All Oregon State Parks** (Ecola, Oswald West, Fort Stevens, Tolovana, Arcadia, Nehalem): **leashed, 6 ft.**
- **Gorge + Mt. Hood trails:** leashed dogs welcome on nearly all of them.
- **Indoors is the recurring "no":** dogs can't go inside Pittock Mansion, Timberline Lodge, the Astoria Column tower, Powell's, Lan Su Garden, the Japanese Garden, or the Oregon Zoo. Plan to tag-team (one waits outside with the pups).

---

## ⚠️ Act-on-these 2026 logistics
- **Multnomah Falls timed permit** — required to use the I-84 Exit 31 lot **9am–6pm, daily, May 22–Sep 7**. **$2 on Recreation.gov**, released 14 days out (7am PT) + a 2-days-out batch. **Easiest workaround: arrive before 9am or after 6pm — no permit needed.** Free walk-up permits also at the Troutdale visitor center / Cascade Locks museum.
- **Fort Stevens day-use permit** — now mandatory park-wide (since Oct 2025): **$10/day or $30 annual Oregon State Parks pass.** The same Oregon pass covers **Ecola's $5** day-use too — worth buying the annual.
- **Northwest Forest Pass (annual, $30)** — smart buy for Mt. Hood: covers Little Zigzag, Mirror Lake, Old Salmon River **and** substitutes for **Trillium Lake's $10** fee (a single-day NW Forest pass does NOT cover Trillium).
- **⚠️ Hug Point is CLOSED** — emergency erosion closure since Nov 2025, **no reopening timeline** as of May 2026. Don't build the Saturday around its waterfall/sea caves. *(Verify before assuming reopened.)*
- **Ecola State Park REOPENED April 2026** after a landslide — open, but the **west leg of the Clatsop Loop Trail stays closed.**
- **Sleepy Monk Coffee café is closed** to walk-ins (roasting only) — drop it.

## Day-by-day: ADD / SWAP / DROP

### Wed 7/22 — Portland
**Keep:** Downtown, Pittock (grounds only w/ dogs), Rose Garden (leashed OK).
- **ADD — Forest Park / Washington Park trails:** the Wildwood Trail links Pittock ↔ Washington Park; all leashed-dog OK and right by your existing stops. Easy way to tire out the dogs.
- **ADD — a dog-friendly food-cart pod:** Cartopia (SE Hawthorne, covered), Hawthorne Asylum, or Cartlandia (beer garden). Leashed dogs welcome at the outdoor picnic seating.
- **ADD — a brewpub patio:** Lucky Lab (the OG dog brewpub), Breakside, or StormBreaker — all have water-bowl dog patios.
- **EAT — Salt & Straw** ice cream (order at the window, enjoy on the bench with the pups).
- **DROP for dog days:** Lan Su Chinese Garden, Powell's interior, Japanese Garden (service animals only). One person can pop into Powell's while the other walks dogs.

### Thu 7/23 — Gorge waterfalls
**Keep:** Vista House, Latourell (best dog waterfall walk), Bridal Veil, Wahkeena, Multnomah, Horsetail — all leashed-OK.
- **ADD — Bonneville Fish Hatchery (Herman the Sturgeon):** free, easy, genuinely fun; 10-ft, 500-lb sturgeon + trout ponds. Leashed dogs welcome on the hatchery grounds.
- **ADD — Cascade Locks + Bridge of the Gods + Thunder Island**, then lunch at **Thunder Island Brewing** — riverfront dog patio with water bowls and an actual dog menu. (Your itinerary already floats Cascade Locks as an optional lunch — this is the pick.)
- **ADD (off-leash romp) — Sandy River Delta** in Troutdale on the way out/back: rare large **off-leash** area (1,400 acres). *⚠️ Verify gate/parking day-of — part of the parcel is under restoration closure, but the main off-leash rec area is open.*
- **OPTIONAL detour east — Hood River:** most dog-friendly Gorge town (waterfront trail, pFriem/Ferment/Double Mountain patios, Fruit Loop stands). Adds time; only if you want a bigger day.
- **SCENIC, dogs-stay-roadside — Rowena Crest** overlook (gorgeous), but **dogs are banned on the Tom McCall Preserve trails** — viewpoint photo only.

### Fri 7/24 — Mt. Hood → Cannon Beach (transition day)
**Reality check:** Portland→Hood ≈ 75–90 min; Hood→Cannon Beach ≈ 3 hrs. **~4–4.5 hrs driving** before stops — pick **2–3 short stops max**, all strung along US-26.
**Keep:** Timberline (walk the grounds w/ dogs — no inside/patio dining), Trillium Lake loop.
- **ADD — Little Zigzag Falls** (best-value stop): flat 0.6-mi shaded round-trip (~20 min) right off US-26. Leashed-OK, cool on a hot day. *(NW Forest Pass.)*
- **ADD (alt) — Old Salmon River Trail** (Welches): easy old-growth riverside walk, go as far as you like. Leashed, dog-loved.
- **EAT — Government Camp:** Mt. Hood Brewing (seasonal dog deck + treats) or Glacier Public House (dog patio). Sandy has dog-patio lunch spots (Le Happy crêpes, Paola's Pizza Barn) if you'd rather stop lower down.
- **SKIP this day (too far / not dog-suitable):** Lost Lake (1.5–2 hrs each way off-route), Ramona Falls (sketchy log river-crossing, bad with small dogs), Mirror Lake (a real 2–2.5 hr hike — do it only if you skip other stops).

### Sat 7/25 — Cannon Beach coast day
**Keep:** Haystack Rock, Ecola, Indian Beach, Oswald West/Short Sand, Neahkahnie, Wayfarer.
- **⭐ ANCHOR the morning on the low-tide window** at Haystack Rock for tide-pooling (leashed): arrive ~1–1.5 hrs before low tide; the best summer minus-tides are mornings near the new/full moon. *⚠️ Get exact July 24–26 times day-of from cannonbeach.org / NOAA — I couldn't pull confirmed times.*
- **SWAP for the now-closed Hug Point → Tolovana Beach + Arcadia Beach:** Tolovana (south end, free lot, big sand, full Haystack view, **off-leash** city-beach jurisdiction) and Arcadia (quiet cove ~3 mi south, leashed) cover the same "pretty beach access" without Hug Point's closure.
- **ADD — Manzanita half-day** (~20 min south): arguably the most dog-centric town on the coast — 7-mi flat dog beach, water bowls everywhere, dog-friendly eats (Manzanita News & Espresso garden patio, San Dune Pub, Sisters & Pete's ice cream). Best escape if downtown Cannon Beach is mobbed on Saturday.
- **ADD (evening) — Seaside Promenade** (~15 min north): flat 1.5-mi oceanfront boardwalk, very dog-friendly evening stroll.
- **EAT in Cannon Beach (dog patios):** **Public Coast Brewing** (best dog patio — bowls + treats) · **Pelican Brewing** · **Bill's Tavern** (4 outdoor tables, order inside) · **Suzy's Scoops** ice cream · **Bruce's Candy Kitchen** · coffee at **Elk Creek** or **Bald Eagle** (not Sleepy Monk — closed).
- **SKIP with small dogs — Saddle Mountain:** upper trail has exposed wire-mesh matting that catches paws + steep slick rock.
- **⚠️ Oswald West note:** main beach trail open; **upper Short Sand Creek Trail (Trail 12) closed** (bridge). Park in the US-101 lots before 10am or after 1pm; ~0.5-mi walk down.

### Sun 7/26 — Astoria finale → PDX (TIGHT)
**Reality check:** Astoria→PDX is **~2 hrs via US-30**. To return the car ~2:45 and fly 5:39, **leave Astoria by ~12:15–12:30.** That's roughly a **9am–12:30 window** — 2–3 stops, not six.
**Keep:** Peter Iredale shipwreck (leashed-OK beach), Astoria.
- **ADD — Astoria Column** first thing: best Columbia panorama; leashed dogs on the grounds (one climbs the tower, one holds the pups). $10/yr parking pass. Lowest crowds early.
- **ADD — Astoria Riverwalk** stroll (leashed, bag stations) + **Coffee Girl** or **Bowpicker Fish & Chips** (famous tuna-and-chips; opens 11am Sun, expect a line — only if you're leaving on the later side).
- **EAT breakfast (dog-OK):** **Blue Scorcher Bakery** (dog sidewalk seating, open Sun 7am) or **Pig 'N Pancake** takeout.
- **OPTIONAL — Fort Clatsop / Lewis & Clark NHP:** flat forest trails, leashed-OK, and it's on the PDX side of town — works *only if you drop something else.*
- **Quick photo — Goonies house:** street-view only (private residence), 5–10 min drive-by.
- **🚦 Cape Disappointment — SKIP if tight (recommended):** it's across the river in WA (wrong direction from PDX), needs a **separate WA Discover Pass**, is **partially closed for renovation through ~June 2026**, and realistically eats the whole morning. Save it for a trip where it's your *first* stop, not your last.
- **⚠️ Astoria Riverfront Trolley:** was under major repair early 2026, "hoping to reopen in spring" — **don't count on it; check old300.org day-of.**

## Quick "best adds" shortlist
1. **Bonneville Hatchery (Herman)** + **Thunder Island Brewing** patio on the Gorge day.
2. **Little Zigzag Falls** as the easy Mt. Hood-day dog walk.
3. **Tolovana/Arcadia** to replace closed Hug Point; **Public Coast Brewing** for the signature dog meal.
4. **Manzanita** as the dog-town half-day swap if Cannon Beach is crowded.
5. **Astoria Column first thing Sunday**, then skip Cape Disappointment to protect the flight.

## Passes to buy ahead
- **Oregon State Parks annual ($30)** → covers Fort Stevens + Ecola.
- **Annual Northwest Forest Pass ($30)** → covers Mt. Hood stops incl. Trillium.
- **Multnomah Falls timed permit ($2)** if you'll be there 9am–6pm — or just go early/late.
- **Astoria Column ($10/yr vehicle)** — bought on-site.
