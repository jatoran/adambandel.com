## Trip structure

**Flights**

- **Arrive:** Wed 7/22, PDX, 9:25 a.m.
- **Depart:** Sun 7/26, PDX, 5:39 p.m.

**Hotels**

- **Wed night:** Portland
- **Thu night:** Portland
- **Fri night:** Cannon Beach
- **Sat night:** Cannon Beach

---

# Wednesday 7/22 — Portland first

**Theme:** easy arrival day, light city stuff before nature ruins cities for you.

1. Land at **PDX**
2. Pick up rental car
3. Check bags / early check-in if possible
4. **Downtown Portland**
5. **Pittock Mansion viewpoint**
6. **International Rose Test Garden**
7. Dinner in Portland
8. Sleep in **Portland**

---

# Thursday 7/23 — Waterfall Highway / Columbia River Gorge

**Theme:** “waterfall highway” day trip from Portland.

1. Leave Portland
2. **Vista House / Crown Point**
3. **Latourell Falls**
4. **Bridal Veil Falls**
5. **Wahkeena Falls**
6. **Multnomah Falls**
7. **Horsetail Falls**
8. Optional: lunch / stop in **Cascade Locks** or nearby
9. Return to Portland
10. Sleep in **Portland**

**Note:** reserve/plan around Multnomah Falls permit timing.

---

# Friday 7/24 — Mt. Hood to Cannon Beach

**Theme:** mountain views, then move to the coast.

1. Check out of Portland hotel
2. Drive toward Mt. Hood
3. **Timberline Lodge / Mt. Hood area**
4. **Trillium Lake**
5. Drive to **Cannon Beach**
6. Check into Cannon Beach hotel
7. **Haystack Rock / Cannon Beach sunset**
8. Sleep in **Cannon Beach**

---

# Saturday 7/25 — Cannon Beach coast day

**Theme:** best coastal views, no major driving pressure.

1. **Wayfarer Restaurant** breakfast
2. **Haystack Rock / Cannon Beach**
3. **Ecola State Park**
4. **Indian Beach**
5. **Oswald West State Park / Short Sand Beach**
6. **Neahkahnie Viewpoint**
7. Return to Cannon Beach
8. Sunset / beach walk
9. Sleep in **Cannon Beach**

---

# Sunday 7/26 — North coast / optional Washington / fly home

**Theme:** scenic final day, but protect airport timing.

1. Check out of Cannon Beach
2. **Fort Stevens / Peter Iredale shipwreck**
3. **Astoria**
4. Optional: **Cape Disappointment, Washington**
5. Drive back to **PDX**
6. Return rental car around **2:45–3:00 p.m.**
7. Depart PDX at **5:39 p.m.**

**If Sunday feels too tight:** skip Cape Disappointment and just do Cannon Beach → Astoria → PDX.
