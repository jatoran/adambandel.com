# Ground

`h_*.bin` (heights), `l_*.webp` (the starlight that reaches each point, the snow) and `ground.json` are made by `tools/globular-bake.py` from the U.S. Geological Survey's 3D Elevation Program (3DEP), the basin of Thousand Island Lake, Sierra Nevada:

- Source: https://elevation.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevation/ImageServer (fetched by `tools/globular-fetch.py`).
- Public domain (U.S. Geological Survey).

Made: the lake's bed, the islets, the roughness finer than the survey, the light, the snow.
