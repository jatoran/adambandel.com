# Notice

White Room contains Abyssal Garden by Maksymilian Dendura (https://github.com/Noniv/abyssal-garden), used under the MIT License, and builds on its renderer.

## The world behind the blue door

The underwater world behind the blue door is the scene of Abyssal Garden, brought over from its source.
Everything in `src/abyss/` is that scene's code, adapted to stand behind a doorway:

- `src/abyss/layout.js`: the scene's layout tables, viewpoints and camera path.
- `src/abyss/world.js`: its world generator.
- `src/abyss/meshes.js`: its procedural meshes (rocks, sponges, coral, the whale fluke, the wreck, the manta, the fish).
- `src/abyss/wgsl.js`: its shaders (terrain density, caustics, materials, kelp, animals, water surface, particles, light shafts).
- `src/abyss/renderer.js`: its draw calls, draw distances and levels of detail.
- `src/abyss/build.js`: its build steps and their order.
- `src/player.js`: its swimming (speeds, how the body takes them up, sliding along rock).
- `src/wgsl/post.js`: its grade, applied where the underwater world shows.

What White Room adds to that world is the blue door: the door and the plinth it stands on, the pipelines that draw the world through the doorway, the doorway's light in the water, and the water laid over the room seen from it.
Nothing is generated where the door stands; the rest of the world comes out as it does in Abyssal Garden.

## The jungle and the room

These parts of the renderer for the room and the jungle are derived from Abyssal Garden, and are marked in the source files that contain them:

- `src/math.js`: seeded random streams, value noise, quaternion helpers.
- `src/geometry.js`: surface nets, the trilinear sampler, the mesh builder core, the icosphere.
- `src/world/terrain.js` and `src/wgsl/density.js`: the density pipeline and the rock formulas.
- `src/wgsl/textures.js`: the detail texture and the bloom chain.
- `src/wgsl/solid.js`: triplanar detail and layered rock.
- `src/wgsl/leaf.js`: the instanced strip that the vertex shader bends and sways.
- `src/wgsl/post.js`: the depth-aware blur and upsample, the tone mapping.
- `src/renderer.js`: the frame structure, cell culling, GPU timing and dynamic resolution.

| Use | Source | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| The air over the sea: one HDRI panorama (Kloppenheim 06, Pure Sky) | [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/license), CC0 1.0 | `assets/worldedge/sky/CREDITS.md` names the asset, its authors and its link |
| The Milky Way: the panorama eso0932a, credit ESO/S. Brunier | [ESO](https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso0932a/), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) | `assets/worldedge/sky/CREDITS.md` |
| The stars | Yale Bright Star Catalogue, 5th revised edition (Hoffleit and Warren 1991, CDS V/50), as `src/moon/stars-data.js` holds it | `src/moon/stars-data.js` |
| 2 texture sets: layered dark rock, rock with lichen | [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/license), CC0 1.0 | `assets/worldedge/tex/CREDITS.md` names each asset, its authors and its link |
| 7 recordings: the top of a large waterfall, Niagara, rapids, boots on gritty ground | [Freesound](https://freesound.org/), CC0 1.0 | `assets/worldedge/audio/CREDITS.md` names each recording, its author and its link |

The sky and the Milky Way were resampled by `tools/worldedge-sky.py`, the textures by `tools/place-textures.py` from `tools/worldedge-textures.json`; the recordings were cut and levelled by `tools/place-audio.py` from `tools/worldedge-audio.json`.
What the page makes itself: the rim, the sea, the falling water, the spray and its rainbow, the rock's shapes, and the carving over the portal.

## The Stopped Storm

This section is the place's part of the page's `NOTICE.md`.
Its sound is cut from two recordings published under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication), kept as files beside the page in `assets/thunderstorm/`.

| Use | Source | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| A thunderstorm's rain with a thunderclap, played slowed | [BigSoundBank, Rain and Thunder #2](https://bigsoundbank.com/rain-and-storm-2-s0740.html) by Joseph Sardin, CC0 1.0 | `assets/thunderstorm/audio/CREDITS.md` |
| A storm sea, played slowed | [Freesound, Rough Baltic Sea](https://freesound.org/people/GirlWithSoundRecorder/sounds/441008/) by GirlWithSoundRecorder, CC0 1.0 | `assets/thunderstorm/audio/CREDITS.md` |

Photographs from Wikimedia Commons and the NOAA Photo Library (lightning over the sea at night, the inside of a breaking wave, a storm sea) were looked at to judge the picture and are not part of the page; `docs/design/thunderstorm.md` names them.

## The Sea Overhead

The seabed's surfaces are photographed texture sets published under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication), and its ocean ambience is cut from CC0 hydrophone recordings.
They are kept as files beside the page, in `assets/seaoverhead/`.

| Use | Source | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| 4 texture sets: rippled beach sand, shell floor, coastal limestone rock, porous coral ground | [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/license) (CC0 1.0) | `assets/seaoverhead/tex/CREDITS.md` names each asset, its authors and its link |
| 2 hydrophone ocean recordings: surging underwater swells and deep oceanic ambience | [Freesound](https://freesound.org/) (CC0 1.0) | `assets/seaoverhead/audio/CREDITS.md` names each recording, its author and its link |

The textures were resampled and repacked by `tools/place-textures.py` from `tools/seaoverhead-textures.json`, and the audio excerpts were cut and levelled by `tools/place-audio.py` from `tools/seaoverhead-audio.json`.
The seabed terrain, reef outcrops, giant kelp stands, hanging ocean ceiling, overhead fish schools, manta rays, sunbeams and dry-sand footsteps are made by the page.

## The Bismuth Canyon

The canyon's surface detail textures and ambient/footstep recordings are published by their authors under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication).
They are kept as files beside the page, in `assets/bismuth/`.

| Use | Source | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| 4 texture sets: smooth silver metal, pitted raw metal, dark fractured rock, layered dark rock | [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/license) and [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com/), CC0 1.0 | `assets/bismuth/tex/CREDITS.md` names each asset, its authors and its link |
| 6 recordings: desert canyon wind, a courtyard's impulse response (the canyon's echo), 4 hard-stone footsteps | [Freesound](https://freesound.org/), CC0 1.0 | `assets/bismuth/audio/CREDITS.md` names each recording, its author and its link |

## The stars over the moon

The positions, magnitudes and colour indices of the stars brighter than magnitude 6.5 in the moon's sky are from the Yale Bright Star Catalogue, 5th Revised Edition (D. Hoffleit and W. H. Warren Jr., 1991), distributed by the Centre de Données astronomiques de Strasbourg as catalogue V/50.
`tools/make-stars.mjs` packs them into `src/moon/stars-data.js`; the catalogue itself is not part of this repository.
