# Notice

White Room builds on the renderer architecture of Abyssal Garden by Maksymilian Dendura (https://github.com/Noniv/abyssal-garden), used under the MIT License.

Parts derived from it are marked in the source files that contain them:

- `src/math.js`: seeded random streams, value noise, quaternion helpers.
- `src/geometry.js`: surface nets, the trilinear sampler, the mesh builder core, the icosphere.
- `src/world/terrain.js` and `src/wgsl/density.js`: the density pipeline and the rock formulas.
- `src/wgsl/textures.js`: the detail texture and the bloom chain.
- `src/wgsl/solid.js`: triplanar detail and layered rock.
- `src/wgsl/leaf.js`: the instanced strip that the vertex shader bends and sways.
- `src/wgsl/post.js`: the depth-aware blur and upsample, the tone mapping.
- `src/renderer.js`: the frame structure, cell culling, GPU timing and dynamic resolution.
