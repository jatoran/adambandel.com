⚡ The short version

  • Reserve a “Standard SUV” (2-row) for cargo margin, or an Intermediate SUV to save — both swallow 2 bags + a large folded stroller with the dogs on the back seat. You book a class, not a model, so you’ll get “X or similar.”
  • Best to hope for / request: Hyundai Tucson or Honda CR-V (most cargo), or a Subaru Outback/Forester if you want AWD + the easiest dog loading. All six picks below have lane-keeping + adaptive cruise standard.
  • Cheapest path: book a refundable Costco Travel rate now and set an AutoSlash price-tracker. Realistic peak-July all-in: ~$350–470 (Intermediate) / ~$450–600 (Standard) for the 4 days, incl. PDX’s ~28–30% taxes/fees.
  • Dogs: every brand allows them with no upfront fee — but return it vacuumed & hair-free or risk a $50–250 (up to ~$450) cleaning fee. Bring a back-seat/cargo cover.
  • Book now — these are peak-summer dates only ~a month out; SUVs and the best rates go first.

Notes & fine print

  • Prices are estimates for Jul 22–26, 2026 and include the PDX fee stack — Oregon has no sales tax, but Multnomah County adds a 17% rental tax plus a ~11% concession-recovery fee and facility charges (~28–30% total on airport pickups). Picking up off-airport avoids the concession fee (~10–17% less) but adds a trip into town.
  • You reserve a class, not a model. “Intermediate SUV” / “Standard SUV” bookings come as “[model] or similar.” The 6 cars above are what to hope for; National’s Emerald Aisle lets you pick any SUV in the aisle on the spot, and Turo lets you choose the exact model.
  • Driver-assist isn’t guaranteed by class — but every model above has lane-keeping + adaptive cruise standard on all trims, so any example you’re handed will have it. If you want to be sure, Turo lets you pick a specific car, or ask at the counter.
  • Dogs: no brand charges an upfront pet fee, but all reserve the right to bill a cleaning/detailing fee ($50–250, up to ~$450 reported at Avis/Hertz) for leftover hair/odor. Use a seat/cargo cover, secure the dogs, and vacuum + lint-roll before return. Service animals are exempt. Hertz can provide a pet barrier grate if you ask ~24h ahead.
  • Turo is peer-to-peer (no counter — meet the host or arrange airport delivery); pet rules are per-host, so message the host before booking to confirm 2 dogs are OK. Fox and Nu Car are not official on-site PDX partners (broker/shuttle) — skip unless dramatically cheaper and you confirm pickup logistics.
