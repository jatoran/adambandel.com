Portland

Wed Jul 22 → Fri Jul 24, 2026 · 2 nights

Base for the arrival day + the Columbia Gorge waterfall day. You want a walkable, safe, pretty neighborhood (downtown core / West End / waterfront) with room for the pups. Portland's boutique scene is very dog-welcoming — several here charge no pet fee at all.

Cannon Beach

Fri Jul 24 → Sun Jul 26, 2026 · 2 nights

Base for the two coast days (Haystack Rock, Ecola, Oswald West). Cannon Beach is one of the most dog-friendly towns on the coast — dogs run off-leash on the main beach. Peak-summer oceanfront rooms get pricey; the ⚠️ resorts are the prettiest but usually top $300, so they're flagged.

How to read this & what to verify

  • Prices are estimates for Jul 22–24 (Portland) and Jul 24–26 (Cannon Beach), based on current/typical 2026 summer rates — rooms aren't bookable this far out. The Hotels.com links are pre-pointed at each property; plug in the dates to see live rates.
  • Pet fees & taxes are on top of the nightly room rate shown. City/lodging tax runs ~15% in Portland and ~10–11% on the coast. A few Portland hotels (The Nines, Hi-Lo) also add a mandatory nightly destination fee — flagged on the card.
  • Always book the designated pet room and re-confirm the 2-dog allowance when you reserve — every property limits dogs to specific rooms, and a couple (Surfsand, Tolovana) exclude their very top oceanfront units from pet stays.
  • Room types & sizes (the "🛏 Room types & sizes" panel on each card) come from each hotel's own site/booking data: square footage, bed setup, view, occupancy, features, and an estimated per-room nightly rate. Where a hotel only allows dogs in certain rooms, the non-pet room types are flagged 🚫. Per-room rates are estimates and step up by tier — the live per-room price for your dates shows on the Hotels.com link.
  • Dog policies were cross-checked against each hotel's own site plus BringFido. All photos (hotel + room) are saved locally in hotel_images/ so this page works offline; the Hotels.com galleries have full, current photo sets.
